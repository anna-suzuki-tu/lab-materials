Knowledge Map

Knowledge Map — What you need to know to advance your research. By when, and for what
Once you start writing a Paper Card, you hit places you cannot write. You cannot write the Field Belief because you lack the basics of the field. You cannot pose an estimation-type question because you lack the way of thinking of inverse problems. This map is the place to come back to when you get stuck.

How to use

  • Each item says "where it matters on the Paper Card." Look it up backward from the stage where you are stuck
  • "Undergraduate course" corresponds to the curriculum of the Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering. Fill in courses you missed by self-study
  • "By when" is a guide. You need not acquire everything as a B4. B4: the foundation and the basics of your own team; M: specialization; D: crossing over
  • One book per item. It means "if in doubt, start here" — it does not exclude others

(★ = course or book name still being finalized. To be filled in progressively)

IV Crossing over: what to acquire as a D

  • The other team's foundation: Team Rock students learn the free energy principle; Team Society students learn inverse problems. Understand, at the mathematical level, that both teams share the same form of question — "estimate invisible structure from limited observations"
  • Cross-disciplinary question archetypes (03-A): practice finding them in papers outside your own field
  • Research funding and ethics: Research Funding & Budget lecture (in preparation)
