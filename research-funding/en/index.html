Act 2
What it can be used for
Permitted uses and procedures

Permitted uses and procedures

  • Goods (consumables, equipment), travel, personnel and honoraria, other (publication fees, conference registration)
  • ★ How the lab budget is used (whom to consult, how to order)
  • Do not go to the store yourself to order. ★ The procedure through the administrative office (Ms. Otomo)
  • The fiscal-year wall: there is a constraint that funds cannot be used in March. Order by counting backwards
Act 3
Applying
Funding students can apply for

Funding students can apply for

  • JSPS DC1/DC2: apply in M2 or D1. Framing-A/B of the Paper Card becomes the skeleton of the research plan as is (Core Question, Compared to X we Y, Key Contributions)
  • ★ On-campus student grants, foundation grants (list and timing)
  • Write the application on the assumption that "the reviewer reads it in five minutes." The principles of Design for Research apply
  • Even if you are not selected, what you wrote is not wasted. The Paper Card gets stronger
The skeleton of the research plan is Framing-A/B of the Paper Card. Even if you are not selected, the Paper Card gets stronger.
Act 4
Budget ethics
What must not be done

What must not be done

  • Use outside the purpose: do not use one project's money for another project
  • Personal use: goes without saying
  • Relations with vendors: deposited funds, fictitious travel, fictitious honoraria are treated the same as research misconduct
  • If in doubt, ask before spending. If you ask after spending, it sometimes cannot be undone
  • ★ The university's handbook on the use of research funds (link)
If in doubt, ask before spending. After spending, it sometimes cannot be undone.

Travel and purchases
Practicalities

The practicalities of travel and purchases

  • Travel procedures are in Notion Lab OS "Administration (AA, travel)." Apply in advance; settle within two weeks after attending
  • Registration fees and travel expenses are, in principle, paid out of pocket and reimbursed. If that is difficult, consult Suzuki early
  • When goods arrive, inspect and accept them. ★ The acceptance procedure
