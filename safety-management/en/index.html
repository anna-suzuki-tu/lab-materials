Act 1
How to think about safety
Prediction and reporting

Safety management has the same structure as inquiry

Safety management has the same structure as the inquiry of Lab OS Chapter 02. Predict what could happen (hypothesis), compare it with what happened (observation), and update the rules (belief). An accident that was not predicted is a "near miss," and reporting it is the only way to update the rules. We report not to blame, but to prevent the next one.

Reporting is not for blame; it is to prevent the next one.
Act 2
Before the experiment
Approval, two or more people, preparation

Before starting an experiment

  • To ensure safety, always obtain your supervisor's approval before an experiment
  • Experiments are carried out by two or more people. Do not operate equipment alone
  • Take the university's safety training (★ names of required courses, timing) before starting experiments
  • In the experimental plan, write one line each for the anticipated hazards (high temperature, high pressure, chemicals, electricity, heavy objects) and the countermeasures
Act 3
During the experiment
Equipment, chemicals, electricity, high temperature

Rules in the laboratory

  • No eating or drinking in the laboratory
  • Record the amounts of reagents and consumables used
  • Do not repair broken equipment on your own; report it
  • ★ Precautions for each piece of equipment (pressure vessels, furnaces, lasers, etc. — whatever the lab has)
  • ★ Rules for storing and disposing of chemicals (location of SDS, separation of waste liquids)
  • ★ Standards for wearing protective equipment (safety glasses, gloves, lab coat)

When an accident or breakdown occurs
The reporting procedure

The reporting procedure

1
Ensure people's safety
If there is an injury, first aid and emergency services first (★ on-campus contacts)
2
Stop the equipment
If you do not know how to stop it, do not touch it; just cut the power
3
Contact your supervisor
Room 309, ext. 5284, or Slack. Nights and holidays: ★
4
Record it in the Research Log
Record what happened, including near misses (Lab Tools "Research Log & Thinking Log")
5
Share at Team Meeting and update the rules
Share it at the next Team Meeting
Act 5
Information and health safety
Backups, personal data, mind and body

Data, mind, and body are also subjects of safety

  • Back up data at least once a week (Lab Tools "Lab Infrastructure")
  • Store data containing personal information (interviews, questionnaires) according to the conditions of the ethics review. Team Society should take particular care
  • Treat physical and mental ill-health as part of research. Share it at the Individual Meeting. On-campus counseling: ★

Checklist

4
